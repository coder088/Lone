## 🌑 You Were One of Them

You are a **mage**, lost in a medieval world.

The people hunting you aren't monsters.

They are **human**.

They were your companions.

They fought beside you.

Now, they want you dead.

You don't remember why.

**Find out what happened.**

---

## ⚔️ About

**LONE** combines the exploration and progression of a **Metroidvania** with the demanding combat and atmosphere of a **Souls-like**.

Explore a hostile world, uncover hidden paths, master your magic and face the people who once stood beside you.

Every discovery brings you closer to the truth.

And the truth may be worse than death.

---

## 🔮 Magic Is Your Weapon

You play as a **mage**, and magic is at the heart of the gameplay.

Learn, experiment and adapt your abilities to overcome enemies and obstacles.

Power comes with a price.

**Use it wisely.**

---

## 🩸 A World That Remembers

The people you encounter aren't simply obstacles.

They are part of the world.

Some know you.

Some fear you.

Some want you dead.

Their reasons are yours to discover.

# 🛠️ Built Without a Game Engine

This is a **from-scratch project**.

No Unity.
No Unreal.
No Godot.

The game is being written by hand in a code editor using:

| Technology     | Role                        |
| -------------- | --------------------------- |
| **C++**        | Core language               |
| **SFML**       | Multimedia foundation       |
| **Custom C++** | Game systems & architecture |

The goal is simple:

> **Build the game instead of building it inside an engine.**

---

# 👤 One Developer

**LONE is developed completely solo.**

Design, programming, architecture, gameplay systems, debugging and everything in between are handled by one person.

This project is both a game and an exploration of how far a single developer can go with **C++ and SFML**.
